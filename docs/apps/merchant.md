# Merchant — the operator dashboard

The merchant operator's single surface: payments and settlements,
billing and subscriptions, connected accounts, and reporting —
everything the merchant needs to run their commerce operation.

## What it demonstrates

- **One dashboard** — payments, billing, and reporting data side by
  side through typed BFF clients (no direct service access).
- **Operational drill-down** — from a settlement total to the
  individual charges behind it, every level audited.
- **RFW-native** — the merchant-facing surfaces ride the vertical's
  admin definitions where applicable.
