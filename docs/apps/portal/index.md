# Portal — the payer self-service

The payer's own records: invoices, subscription management, payment
methods, and history — self-service without a support ticket.

## What it demonstrates

- **Subscription self-service** — upgrades, downgrades, and cancels
  with proration previewed before confirming.
- **Invoice history** — every invoice with its line items and
  payment status.
- **Payment method management** — add/remove methods; card data
  passes to the delegated rail, never stored in the portal's tables.
